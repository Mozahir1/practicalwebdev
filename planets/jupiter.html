Its Jupiter! The biggest of them all.

How much would you weigh on Jupiter?

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System, a giant ball of gas more than twice as massive as all the other planets combined. Its Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than Earth that has been raging for centuries.

Quick facts

  • Jupiter is about 86,900 miles (139,800 km) across, and about 1,300 Earths could fit inside.
  • It is about 484 million miles from the Sun.
  • One day is just under 10 hours, and one year is almost 12 Earth years.
  • It has more than 90 known moons.
  • The cloud tops are about -234°F (-145°C).
