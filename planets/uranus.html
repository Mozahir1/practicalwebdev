Its Uranus! The tilted one.

↑ North

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun, an ice giant with a pale blue-green color from the methane in its atmosphere. It is tipped over on its side, so it basically rolls around the Sun instead of spinning upright.

Quick facts

  • Uranus is about 31,500 miles (50,700 km) across, about 4 Earths wide.
  • It is about 1.8 billion miles from the Sun.
  • One day is about 17 hours, and one year is 84 Earth years.
  • Its axis is tilted about 98 degrees.
  • It has about 28 known moons, and its atmosphere reaches about -371°F (-224°C).
