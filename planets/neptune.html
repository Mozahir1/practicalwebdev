Its Neptune! The last major planet.

Neptune is the eighth and farthest known major planet from the Sun. It is a deep blue ice giant with the fastest winds in the Solar System, blowing over 1,000 miles per hour. It is so far away that it was found by math before anyone ever saw it.

Quick facts

  • Neptune is about 30,600 miles (49,200 km) across, about 4 Earths wide.
  • It is about 2.8 billion miles from the Sun.
  • One day is about 16 hours, and one year is about 165 Earth years.
  • It has 16 known moons, and the biggest is Triton.
  • The average temperature is about -353°F (-214°C).
