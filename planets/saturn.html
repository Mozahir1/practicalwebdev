Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and famous for its stunning rings, made of countless chunks of ice and rock. It is a gas giant so light that it would float in water, if you could find a bathtub big enough.

Quick facts

  • Saturn is about 72,400 miles (116,500 km) across, about 9 Earths wide.
  • It is about 886 million miles from the Sun.
  • One day is about 10.7 hours, and one year is about 29 Earth years.
  • It has more than 140 known moons, and the biggest is Titan.
  • Its rings are made of ice and rock but are very thin, often only about 30 feet thick.
