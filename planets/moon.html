Its the Moon!

Full Moon

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It is covered in craters and dust, and it is the only other world humans have walked on.

Quick facts

  • The Moon is about 2,159 miles (3,475 km) across, roughly a quarter of Earth's width.
  • It is about 239,000 miles from Earth.
  • It orbits Earth every 27 days, and its phases repeat every 29.5 days.
  • Its gravity is about 1/6 of Earth's.
  • Temperatures swing from about 250°F (120°C) to -208°F (-130°C).
