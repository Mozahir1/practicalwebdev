Its Earth! Home sweet home.

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only place we know of with life. It has liquid water covering most of its surface and an atmosphere that protects us and keeps us breathing.

Quick facts

  • Earth is about 7,900 miles (12,700 km) across.
  • It is about 93 million miles from the Sun.
  • One day is 24 hours and one year is about 365 days.
  • About 71% of its surface is covered in water.
  • It has 1 moon, and the average temperature is about 59°F (15°C).
