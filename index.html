Solar System facts

  • The Solar System formed about 4.6 billion years ago.
  • It has 8 planets: 4 rocky inner planets and 4 giant outer planets.
  • The Sun holds about 99.8% of all the mass in the Solar System.
  • Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth.
  • Jupiter is more than twice as massive as all the other planets combined.
  • Neptune takes about 165 Earth years to go around the Sun once.
  • Only Mercury and Venus have no moons.
  • Voyager 1 is the farthest human-made object from Earth.
